# Fall 2026 Midterm Exam Blueprint

## Evidence and priority key

The exam scope and stated formats below come from page 1 of `Midterm review Fall 2026.pdf` (`MR-P1`). The lecture decks supply the course's terminology and study content; their PDF page numbers are cited below. Matching HTML files are alternate representations of those decks, not additional sources. The textbook is supplementary and does not add exam topics.

Priorities are study recommendations, not exam weights:

- **CRITICAL** — explicitly tied to a case study or diagram, or otherwise requires integrating and applying several concepts.
- **HIGH** — explicitly in scope and tested in more than one stated format, or a foundational process/concept for applying another topic.
- **MEDIUM** — explicitly in scope but listed only for short-answer or other focused knowledge checks.
- **LOW** — no topic is assigned LOW based on the review; absence of evidence is not evidence that an in-scope topic is unimportant.

The review does not state how many questions, points, or minutes each chapter receives. No percentages or exact mock-exam weighting should be inferred.

## Scope guardrails

- The review identifies the assessed modeling topics as **system perspectives/context models** and **use-case diagrams**. Do not assume sequence, class, activity, state, or other UML diagram exercises are in the midterm scope solely because they appear in the modeling deck or activity files.
- The review's Chapter 4 reference to a “user case template” is treated as the lecture's **use-case template**, supported by `Chp4 Requirements Engineering.pdf`, pages 36–45.
- Textbook chapters supplement the lecture topics above. Textbook-only topics are not added to the blueprint.
